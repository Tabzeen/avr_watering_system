### Additional Components
To assemble the casing, the following additional components are required
- 12x M3 Screws ~20mm length
- 6x M3 Screws ~50mm length
- 8x Threaded M3 Screw inserts

### Assembly Instructions

1. Use a Soldering Iron to insert the threaded insert into the each of the mounting holes.
2. For the LCD screen and button Board:
    - Insert M3 screws from the outer side of the enclosure
    - Insert LCD, Buttons and the Button board from the inner side of the enclosure
    - Fasten the button board and the display using M3 nuts.
3. Mount the mainboard and motor board to the bottom plate
4. Slide the top part of the enclosure over the bottom part and screw in the remaining M3 screws
